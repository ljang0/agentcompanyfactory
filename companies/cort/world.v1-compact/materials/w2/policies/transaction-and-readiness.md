External transaction and evidence protocol — September 2026

An instruction requires an instruction ID/idempotency key, exact entity IDs, expected versions, accepted offer reference, item/date intervals, rate and appointment details, authorizing worker and evidence references. The external rental-system actor validates conflicts and authority and returns accepted or rejected status, reason, effective timestamp, before/after values and immutable transaction ID. A repeated idempotency key cannot execute twice. Rejections leave the prior state operative.

Crew reports identify loaded and uncollected IDs, actual times and custody transitions. Receiving confirms actual WH1 arrival. Routine preparation provides the inspection, cleaning and function-check results plus release acknowledgment. Keep the source events distinct.

RC-1: each returned S1, B1 or D1 requires one person-hour and completes at 16:00 on the next business day after actual WH1 receipt when station capacity exists. Residual capacity is three person-hours on September 15, 25 and 28. Passed external checklists support preauthorized routine release; failed checks create holds. Never infer that a future inspection will pass.

A ready backup may be protected while a returned asset is assessed. Any conditional reassignment must be explicitly authored by a worker, name the evidence conditions and preserve the backup until the replacement transaction is acknowledged. The external actor does not choose the allocation.

Billing Intake validates accepted terms against actual collection evidence. Intake acceptance and later ledger posting are separate events. Full LT-1, RV-1 and TM-1 are in the workbook's Shared operating rules.
